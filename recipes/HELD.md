# Recipes held back

These recipes exist in PRISM Studio but are not shipped here: they carry instrument-specific scoring facts
(cut-off values, notes or citations) that may be protected by the rights holder of the instrument.
They are released when their redistribution is verified or when they are stripped of those facts.

* `recipe-psqi.json`: usage notes, transforms and interpretation text with a cut-off for the global score.
* `recipe-tipi.json`: score notes (reverse-coding), transforms and usage text.
* `recipe-wellbeing.json`: a cut-off interpretation text and a citation (References).
* `recipe-wellbeing-multi.json`: versioned scores (VersionedScores) and scoring text for several versions.
